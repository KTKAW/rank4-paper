Box class and unbounded fork continuation
https://d-nb.info/1214302807/34
Warkentin, Exchange graphs via quiver mutation, Chemnitz PhD (2014).
Read the actual thesis sections on forks, diamonds and Example 6.11, not
only the isolated example label. The box-class avoidance proof uses the
inward deletion argument in the recursive construction of Lemma 6.7 and
Definition 6.3. A root or wing has an acyclic deletion at its inward pivot;
a tip is covered by its two earlier wings; fork continuation uses Lemma 2.5.
The paper states these needed properties and explains the induction. These
are published/external mathematical dependencies, not new claims certified
by the Python range checker.

Chelnokov scope audit
https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.01784v1
https://arxiv.org/html/2311.01784v1
The accessible arXiv record has only v1 (3 November 2023), no journal
reference. Read the full argument (Definitions 1--3, Theorem 1, Lemmas 1--4
and concluding remarks), not merely the abstract. The domain is the real
skew-symmetric 4x4 matrices. The 64 orientation/sign regions include their
boundaries; a single function represented on overlapping closed regions
requires consistency of the polynomial values there. Mutation has the
same positive-part convention as the draft.

Theorem 1 asserts that any invariant polynomial on each sign region is a
polynomial in determinant. The proof glues chamber polynomials using
orientation switches and polynomial identities, then introduces continuous
shears and Pfaffian level sets. In its final step, reduction to the square
of the Pfaffian needs evenness under mutation (the Pfaffian changes sign).
Degenerate values in the shear parameter argument also require continuity
or a polynomial-identity extension. Those details are compressed in the
source. Its last remarks assert an integer-input extension via large lattice
points; a careful extension proof is not supplied there.

The draft therefore explicitly assumes the stated external real theorem,
keeps real-mutation invariance and compatible boundary values, and makes no
integer-only extension or independent Lean verification claim. The collision
uses full-support inputs of determinant 4, avoiding a reliance on assigning
a separate boundary value. The conclusion excludes only invariants meeting
those hypotheses and their arbitrary decoders, not all mutation invariants.
Authors should decide whether to retain this conditional proposition or
supply a separate proof of the external theorem's compressed steps.
